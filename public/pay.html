How the numbers work
  1. Budget = your revenue forecast × the payroll % you set (kitchen on its own, and kitchen + service together).
  2. Hourly staff cost hours × rate × the HIGA penalty for the day (Sat 125%, Sun 150%, public holiday 225%; casuals 125/150/175/250%), plus the Mon–Fri evening loading after 7pm.
  3. Salaried staff cost the same every week: salary ÷ 52, posted daily across the days they're rostered.
  4. Actual replaces rostered for days that have happened, from the clock-ins. So the week's number moves from "planned" to "real" as the week goes on.
  5. Full-timers work 38 h a week averaged over 4 weeks = 152 h. Past 152 in the cycle is overtime (1.5× first 2 h, then 2×). Salaried people get a flag instead of a cost.
